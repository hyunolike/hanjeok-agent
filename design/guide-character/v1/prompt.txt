# 잎새 (Ipsae) — Hanjeok guide agent companion character, v1

## Who it is

A small leaf scout. The Hanjeok sun guide stays with the traveller; 잎새 flies
ahead to look something up and comes back with what it found. It is curious,
quick and honest: when it finds nothing it shows empty hands instead of
pretending.

## Must hold in every pose

- Body: ONE rounded leaf, teardrop shaped, tip at the top curling slightly to
  one side. The leaf IS the body and head; there is no separate head.
- Body colour: quiet green #009C75, with one lighter centre vein line.
- Outline: navy #172033, one consistent thick rounded stroke, same weight as the
  reference character's outline.
- Face on the upper half of the leaf: two navy dot eyes, small navy mouth, soft
  coral #F04B3E cheeks at low opacity.
- Limbs: tiny stubby arms and legs in the same green with navy outline.
- One coral #F04B3E ribbon tied at the stem (bottom), tails trailing.
- Any card it holds: warm white #FFFDF8 with navy outline and one small gold
  #F2B84B dot. Gold appears nowhere else.
- NOT round, NOT gold, NO sun rays, NO backpack: it must not be mistaken for the
  sun guide at any size.
- Flat, soft, friendly sticker-like illustration matching the reference
  character's style, proportions of line and level of detail.
- Transparent background. Character centred with margin. No text, no letters,
  no logo, no ground shadow, no scenery, no gradients, no extra characters.
- Square image.

## Poses (one image each, file name = state)

- waiting  — sitting on the ground, legs out, looking up with a calm smile.
- looking  — in flight, leaning forward, one hand shading its eyes, ribbon
             streaming behind.
- found    — standing, holding one card out toward the viewer with both hands,
             pleased.
- choose   — standing, one card in each hand held apart, leaf tip tilted,
             curious expression.
- no-data  — standing, both palms open and empty, looking down at them, mildly
             puzzled. Not sad.
- failed   — sitting, leaf tip drooped forward, small apologetic mouth.
